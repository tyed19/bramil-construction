About

BRAMIL is a new company, but the experience behind it isn't. Before starting out on his own, BRAMIL's owner worked for Dave Greenetz Construction here in Yuba City. Dave has been remodeling homes in this area since 1983, and his company ran on a simple rule: only hire people you'd trust in your own home, and do the work like it's your own house.

Dave is retiring, which is why BRAMIL exists. The work carries over: kitchens, bathrooms, additions, doors and windows. The difference is electrical is in-house now, instead of something that gets handed off to another company.

Most of our work comes from people nearby who were told about us by someone else. We'd like to keep it that way, so estimates are free, prices are put in writing before work starts, and the job site gets cleaned up at the end.

How getting an estimate works

You call, we talk through what you're thinking, and we come out and look at it. That visit is free. After that you get a written price. If you want to go ahead, we agree on a start date and get to work. If the job changes along the way, we talk about it before doing anything that changes the price. That's it. There's no pressure and no obligation from the estimate.

Where we work: Yuba City, Marysville, Olivehurst, Plumas Lake, Sutter, Live Oak, Gridley, and the wider Sacramento area. If you're a little farther out, call anyway and ask.
